# Writing leaderboards

What the public writing boards measured when read, with their snapshot and method. None tests articles or blog posts, and they disagree on first place, so they inform the choice of a flagship-class writer and settle nothing. A user's preference may name a vendor; that user's first-try approvals at the gate are the evidence that can settle it for them.

## Sources

- EQ-Bench, Longform Creative Writing. https://eqbench.com/creative_writing_longform.html (read 2026-10-12). basis: measured. Claude Opus 5, Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Opus 5.5 first to third; snapshot 2026-09-24, the date the board's data file was last modified; method: an eight-turn story of about 1,000 words a turn, scored against a rubric by Claude Sonnet 4.6. Fiction only.
- EQ-Bench, Creative Writing v3, with its About page. https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html and https://eqbench.com/about.html (read 2026-10-12). basis: measured. GPT-6 Astra first, Claude Fable 5.1 second; snapshot 2026-09-24, the date the data file was last modified; method: pairwise matchups judged by Claude Sonnet 4.6, with no control for the judge favouring its own outputs.
- LMArena, Creative Writing leaderboard. https://arena.ai/leaderboard/chat/text/creative-writing (read 2026-10-12). basis: measured. Gemini 4 Argon first, marked preliminary on 1,053 votes, and Claude Opus 5.5 second, within each other's interval; snapshot 2026-10-08; method: anonymous pairwise votes by people.
- LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations, arXiv:2404.13076, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076 (read 2026-10-12). basis: study. Model judges score their own outputs above others' of equal quality, so a board judged by one vendor's model never stands alone for that vendor's models.
