# Key pools, slice 3: build plan (T-452)

Plan: `docs/plans/2026-10-24-key-pools.md` rev 3.1, section 8.2 row 3 ("Probe adapter, claim (KP16), recheck timer, `resumeAt`, Alerts, probe cost shown"). Builds on slice 2 (PR 156, head `2f03418`), stacked on `feat/key-pools-s2`. ADR-0037 gets decisions 25 onward.

## What a probe is (grounded 2026-10-25)

- One `POST {base}/v1/messages` to the pool's probe model, `max_tokens: 1`, one user message of one character, 5 s bound. Headers: `x-api-key` (the docs call it the legacy fallback for `Authorization`, still supported), `anthropic-version` (required; `2026-10-21` is the version the docs list first and recommend; no `temperature`, `top_p` or `top_k` is sent, the three it removed), `content-type`. Sources: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/overview and https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/versioning.
- Never `count_tokens`, never `/v1/models` (KP7: neither is evidence of credit).
- The probe model is data: `probeModel` in `packages/protocol/catalog/key-pools.json` beside the terms (the vendors file has no model prices and its model list is fetched from the network). First-party value: the Haiku-class model, id checked in the models overview; cost from https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing (Haiku 5.5: $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens). One probe is about 10 input tokens and 1 output token: about $0.0000015. The recorded estimate is a fixed `KEY_PROBE_COST_USD` of $0.00002 (a margin that also covers the older, ten times dearer Haiku 4.5), and a probe is made under a gated probe lease, so the member's own budget sees it.
- How often: only an exhausted member whose `nextCheck` is due (default 24 h; at least 1 h; after a probe that finds it dry again the interval doubles to 7 d; a refusal that named a resume date waits for that date), at most one claim at a time per member (30 s), and the inline probe when a pool is empty is the same probe through the same claim. `keypool recheck --member M` probes now but through the same claim: at most one per member per 30 s.

## Steps (tests first and committed red, then the code, then a negative control per guard)

1. **Domain** (`packages/domain/test/key-pool-probe.test.ts`). A member gets `probingUntil` and `failedProbes` (older saved pools load with `null` and `0`). `claimProbe` sets `probingUntil = now + 30 s` and `nextCheck` in one saved write and writes `ProbeClaimed`; refuses a live claim (`claimed`), a member not due (`not_due`), a member that is neither exhausted nor suspected (`not_exhausted`). A probe's answer: still dry doubles the interval (cap 7 d) and raises nothing (KP11); a Messages ok recovers (`KeyRecovered how: probe`, `failedProbes` 0); `resumeAt` wins over the interval; an answer older than `lastChange` changes nothing (KP6). Named: `kp16 a second prober finds the claim and does not probe; a lapsed claim can be taken`, `recheck uses resumeAt, doubles after a failed probe, recovers on a Messages ok`.
2. **Probe adapter** (`apps/connections/test/probe-anthropic.test.ts`, a local fake server). `probe sends max_tokens 1 to the probe model and to the preset's base URL only`; `probe never writes the response text to disk or the alert file`; `no probe path calls count_tokens or the models endpoint`; a timeout or a network error is `unreachable`, never an outcome; the host rule of `baseUrlProblem` applies; the catalog schema pins `probeModel`.
3. **Service** (`apps/connections/test/key-pool-probe-service.test.ts`). The `ProviderProbe` port; `probeMember` and `recheck`: claim first (KP16), probe under a gated lease (a member over budget or off is declined with its gate word and not probed), classify with the pool's dialect (an unmatched 4xx raises one `unclassified` alert of status and kind only), report with `via: "probe"`, the lease closed with the estimate. Named: `N concurrent leases on an empty pool with one due member cause exactly one probe`, `recheck probes only due members`, `a probe answered after a newer report changes nothing`, `a probe's cost is recorded on the member's Connection`. `state` shows the cost of one probe and the probes of the last 24 h.
4. **CLI and timer** (`keypool-cli.test.ts` additions, `keypool-probe-cli.test.ts`, a local fake Messages API that answers per key). `keypool recheck [pool] [--member M]` prints words only; `lease --pool` and `run --pool` on an empty pool with a due member probe once and serve the recovered key; a planted secret in the probe's response body and in the probe's error leaves no trace in files, events, stdout, stderr, alerts. The timer: `apps/connections/units/morndo-keys-recheck.{service,timer}` (a oneshot every hour, 5 minutes after boot, `Persistent=true`), checked by `systemd-analyze verify` and a test of their shape. The updater's install list is not changed in this slice (stated in the PR).
5. **KP18's claimed probe** (left open by slice 2, decision 14): a report inside the hand-off window also claims one probe of the current member; the probe decides it (a dry new key is found at once, a healthy one is left alone). Domain: `claimProbe` also takes a suspected active member. Named: `report within HELPER_TTL of movedAt ... claims one probe of the current member`.

Each step ends with `tsc`, `oxlint`, `scripts/negative-controls.ts --check`; the controls run once at the end on a temporary worktree.

## Not in this slice

The `StopFailure` hook, `write-env` and sinks, `watch` (slice 4); the page card and phone alert (slice 5); installing the timer through the updater.
