Every number is a count of records, recomputed from the stores. A claim counts as later failed when, within 14 days, a check on the same revision failed, its merge was reverted, its deploy rolled back, or its task reopened from done. So a week stays provisional for 14 days after it ends. Time to done runs from a task's first move to in progress to its acceptance as done. Recovery runs from a failed deploy to the next good one on that environment.
