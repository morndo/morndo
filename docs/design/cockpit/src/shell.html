Between briefsMorndo interrupts you only for a decision due before the next brief, or for something stuck that puts a deadline at risk.

Who decides

What agents decide alone, they tell you in the next brief, with Undo
  • It goes out in your nameAn email, an offer, a post, a release
  • It is hard to undoA signed term, a published claim, a deleted record
  • It rests on what only you knowA call you held, a relationship, a promise
  • It stays inside the guardrails and can be undoneA paused ad, a list, a draft, a reminder

A decision is worth your time when you know something the agent does not, or when it cannot be taken back. One you would approve without reading is not: the agent takes it and you can undo it.
